How I lead

Leadership, from the floor up

I lead by giving people a voice and encouraging them to challenge the status quo. Strong teams are built by empowering people to own complex problems, build their own solutions, and explain the reasoning behind their decisions.

People don't grow by executing someone else's plan. They grow by owning the problem — and defending their answer.

Challenge the status quo

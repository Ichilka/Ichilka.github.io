---
title: IT Operations at Manufacturing Scale
category: case-study
featured: true
order: 3
timeframe: Ongoing
role: IT Director
summary: >-
  Running the full technology environment behind a multi-facility
  printing operation — 100+ endpoints, servers, and the systems the
  production floor depends on.
challenge: >-
  A manufacturing IT environment where every workstation, server, and
  switch has a job tied to production. The mandate: keep it all running,
  keep it secure, and keep it moving forward — without a big-company
  budget or headcount.
approach:
  - Standardized endpoint deployment and management so machines are
    rebuildable, not precious.
  - Put monitoring and asset management in place — you can't run what
    you can't see.
  - Built backup and disaster recovery around what production actually
    needs back first, not just what's easiest to restore.
  - Automated repetitive work with PowerShell and deployment tooling so
    the team spends time on problems, not tickets.
outcome:
  - A documented, monitored, recoverable environment across all
    facilities.
  - Faster support response with the same team size.
  - IT positioned as an operations partner, not a cost center.
stack:
  - Windows & Linux
  - Virtualization
  - Monitoring
  - Backup & DR
  - PowerShell

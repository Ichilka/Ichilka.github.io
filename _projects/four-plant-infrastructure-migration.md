---
title: Four-Plant Infrastructure Migration
category: case-study
featured: true
order: 1
timeframe: 6 months
role: IT Director — project owner
summary: >-
  Migrated network and security infrastructure across four manufacturing
  plants in six months — while the presses kept running.
challenge: >-
  Aging, inconsistent network and security infrastructure across four
  production facilities. Downtime in a printing plant isn't an IT metric —
  it's missed press runs and missed customer deadlines. The migration had
  to happen around production schedules, not instead of them.
approach:
  - Mapped every dependency between IT systems and production workflows
    before touching a cable — the plant floor set the schedule, not IT.
  - Standardized the network and firewall architecture across all four
    sites so every plant runs the same playbook.
  - Sequenced cutovers plant-by-plant during production windows, with
    rollback plans tested in advance.
  - Kept plant managers in the loop with plain-language status updates —
    no jargon, no surprises.
outcome:
  - All four plants migrated in six months with minimal disruption to
    business and production operations.
  - One consistent, supportable architecture across every facility.
  - A repeatable migration playbook the team owns and can run again.
stack:
  - Routing & Switching
  - Firewalls
  - VPN / Site-to-Site
  - Network Standardization
---

The lesson from this project: in manufacturing, IT earns trust by
respecting production. Every technical decision was framed around one
question — *what does this mean for the press schedule?* That framing is
why the migration finished on time and why the plant managers now come to
IT early instead of after the fact.
